See what's happening around you, without the internet

Phones running this app pass short reports to each other over radio. No server, no account.

1 · Welcome

What it protects

Works with no internet and no server.
Group reports can only be read by your group.
Reports expire. Your phone keeps no history of where you were.
The ID your phone sends changes every few minutes.

What it doesn't

Someone nearby with the right equipment can tell a phone is running this app.
Public reports can be wrong or fake.
Anyone in your group sees its reports and shared positions.
If your phone is taken unlocked before you wipe, what's on it can be read.

Find phones nearby

To talk to other phones, Android asks you to allow Nearby devices. On some phones it also asks for location: the app never stores or sends your position unless you share it with a group.
