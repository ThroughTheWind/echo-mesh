See what's happening around you, without the internet

Phones running Echo pass short reports to each other over radio. No server, no account.

1 · Welcome

What it protects

Group reports, positions and nicknames can be read only by people who joined in person.
Nothing you send carries your name, number or a fixed ID.
Echo keeps no history of where you or others were. Reports disappear when they expire.
No server, no account, no internet except a map download you start.

What it doesn't

Anyone nearby with a radio receiver can tell your phone is running Echo.
Public reports can be fake, and so can their confirmations.
Anyone in your group can read what's sent to it and pass it on.
If your phone is taken unlocked, whoever has it can read your groups until you wipe it.
