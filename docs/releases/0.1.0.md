# Echo 0.1.0: two phones, one report

The first release, for testing between two phones you hold yourself. It is **not ready for a real
protest**: it has had no outside security review, and the features that protect people in a crowd
(groups, panic wipe, discreet mode) come in later releases.

## What it does

- Phones running Echo find each other over Bluetooth LE, with no internet, server or account.
- You drop a report on the map (police line, medic, water, exit open and others). It is signed,
  expires on its own, and hops from phone to phone to reach people out of your direct range.
- The map works offline, from a map file you download or receive from another phone.
- Settings are stored sealed with a key kept in Android's Keystore.

## Install

1. Download `echo-0.1.0.apk` from this page and check its SHA-256 against the one below.
2. Open it on your phone and allow installing from this source when Android asks.
3. Android 12 or newer. Tested on a Pixel 9a and a OnePlus CPH2465.

## What it does not protect

- Anyone nearby with a radio receiver can tell your phone is running Echo.
- Public reports can be fake, and their confirmations can be faked in bulk. Check before you act
  on one.
- A determined observer with many receivers may guess roughly where a report was first sent.
- Downloading a map reveals the region to your network and the map host.
- There is no panic wipe and no discreet mode yet. Treat a phone with Echo as showing that you
  used it.
- No independent review has checked the code yet (SECURITY.md). Do not rely on it where being
  wrong could hurt you.
